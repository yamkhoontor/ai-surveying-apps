Point Cloud Classification Lab

Scan a simulated street, label a few points, and train an AI to classify the rest into ground, buildings, vegetation, poles and cars. Then see where it goes wrong, and what happens on a street it has never seen.

1 · The street

Street A is 80 m × 40 m: road with kerbs, footpaths, grass, six buildings, trees and shrubs, lamp posts and parked cars. You will label points in the western half (x < 40 m) and the AI will be marked on the eastern half, which it never sees.
