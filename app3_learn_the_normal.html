Learn the Normal

A structure breathes with the temperature every day. Let a model learn each prism's normal daily behaviour from the weeks before excavation, then watch what is left: real movement, measured against the Alert and Work Suspension Levels.

1 · The site

Eight prisms on a building beside an excavation, read every hour for 70 days by a robotic total station. Excavation starts on day 45. Prisms 3, 4 and 5 then settle; prism 6 drops suddenly on day 58. The others do not move.
