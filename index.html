AI for Surveying and Geomatics · Companion apps

Three AI laboratories for surveyors

Each app trains a real AI model inside your browser on simulated survey data, so the right answer is known and the AI can be marked honestly. Use them online in any web browser, or download the package and use them offline: no installation, no account, and no internet connection needed once downloaded. They work on a computer, tablet or phone.

Chapter 7

App 1 — Point Cloud Classification Lab

Scan a simulated street, label a few hundred points and train a random forest or a neural network to classify ground, buildings, vegetation, poles and cars. Inspect the errors in 3D, read the confusion matrix, and test the model on a street it has never seen.
Chapter 9

App 2 — GNSS Urban Canyon

Train a classifier to recognise reflected-only (NLOS) satellite signals from their strength, elevation and least squares residual, then walk a street and compare position errors with and without the AI.
Chapter 10

App 3 — Learn the Normal

Let a model learn each prism's daily thermal behaviour before excavation, and separate real settlement from the structure's breathing. Compare alarms from raw readings, daily means and the AI against the Alert and Work Suspension Levels.
